### Foreword

History Gallery of SeMA Bunker is dedicated to the preservation and construction of the identity of SeMA Bunker. The gallery exhibits materials related to the site to remind of its history and reexamine its past. At the same time, the gallery commissions video works that deal with SeMA Bunker and its archive. It is hoped that SeMA Bunker Archive Project transforms SeMA Bunker into a site for continued imagination and production and builds a new kind of archive which offers thoughts and ideas that will emerge in the coming time. SeMA Bunker aspires to achieve the goal by focusing on the future, rather than approaching itself as a mere depository of records and documents.

* from: Seoul Museum of Art
