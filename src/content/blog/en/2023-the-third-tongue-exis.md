### Introduction

The programming section EX-Choice aims to approach the contexts of contemporary moving images and experimental film from diverse perspectives and to introduce trends that deserve attention going forward. Each year it arranges films under a specific theme, covering a wide range from historical viewpoints to innovative aspects. The theme for 2023 is “Korean Experimental Film 2004–2022.” Major Korean works screened during the 1st through 17th editions of EXiS have been re-arranged into five programs.
