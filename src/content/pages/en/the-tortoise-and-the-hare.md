### SYNOPSIS

**The Tortoise and the Hare** skillfully subverts the timeless 'wisdom' derived from the Aesop fable. Through this subversion, it confronts the notion of challenging dominant discourse and explores the inescapable reinscription that occurs within this very process of subversion.
