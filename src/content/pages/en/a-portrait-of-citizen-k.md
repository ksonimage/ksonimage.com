### SYNOPSIS

"No one knew that one day, Citizen K had turned into a bird."

_**A Portrait of Citizen K**_ chronicles the life and death of a middle-aged man named K, who fades into obscurity. Blending fictional and documentary elements, the film delves into urban spaces, tracing from K’s sole record of existence - his residential registration number.
