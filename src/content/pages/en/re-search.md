### SYNOPSIS

A researcher's blind struggle for her thesis inside the engulfing white space creates the uncertainty of her own research.

### REVIEW

"With a remarkable sense of humour and understatement, the filmmaker condenses a whole day's worth of research into twenty short minutes." - Visions du Réel
