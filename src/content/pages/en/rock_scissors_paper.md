### SYNOPSIS

Immersed in a profound sense of déjà vu, **ROCK SCISSORS PAPER** transports us to the enchanting realm of children, where the present moment reigns supreme. Within this nostalgic journey, it retrieves memories of childhood and intertwines them with the tapestry of adult experiences. Moreover, it discerns the cyclical echoes of the children's future within the remnants of past cultures, captured within tangible artifacts.
