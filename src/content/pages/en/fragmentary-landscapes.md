### SYNOPSIS

The distant, elusive recollection of barren landscapes across the United States during the years 2000 to 2003 unveils a series of portraits capturing the essence of everyday life suspended in a perpetual state of uncertainty and ambiguity.

### REVIEW

[A study on the image of ‘landscape’ and emotional thinking in two ‘landscape of fragments’](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART002914894) - CHO, Eun Joo / Korean Literature, Jeonbuk National University
